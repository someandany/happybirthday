=========================================================
HOW TO ADD YOUR BIRTHDAY PHOTOS HERE
=========================================================

Place your photo files right inside this folder (`images/`):

1. PAGE 1 - Pop-Out Photo (pops out of the lily when it finishes blooming):
   - Name your file: `popout.jpg`
   (or if it's a PNG: `popout.png`, change the extension in the HTML)

2. PAGE 2 - Top Slideshow Photos (shown in the memories carousel):
   - Photo 1: `photo1.jpg`
   - Photo 2: `photo2.jpg`
   - Photo 3: `photo3.jpg`
   - Photo 4: `photo4.jpg`

You can add more photos, change captions, or change file names anytime!
Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif
